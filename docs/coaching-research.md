# What chess coaches & players actually need (research → features)

Research distilled into how Strategem should help. Sources at the bottom.

## What strong coaches look for (not single moves — decisions & patterns)
- **Recurring patterns over one-off blunders.** Most mistakes come from the *same*
  thought-process error repeated. Coaches hunt the pattern, then build a plan around it.
  → Strategem: **"Mistakes they repeat"** + **mindset signals** (Analytics) + the
  **top recurring error** drives focus area #2 (Curriculum).
- **The six core aspects:** opening edge / recovery, tactics & blunder prevention,
  endgame conversion, **time management**, planning, and consistency vs peers.
  → Strategem: phase strengths, tactical motifs, consistency score, mindset signals.
- **A study plan + accountability.** The coach's job is diagnosis → plan → encouragement.
  → Strategem: **Curriculum tab** = prioritized focus areas + a realistic weekly routine.
- **Deeper than tactics:** poor planning and positional misunderstanding hide behind
  "tactical" losses. → phase accuracy + "find the plan" drills in the middlegame track.

## What truly helps players improve
- **Deliberate practice, little and often** beats cramming → weekly routine, 3 short
  tactic sets, one deep self-review.
- **Review your own games**, guessing the move before checking → built into the plan.
- **Targeted tactics** on the patterns you miss (not random puzzles) → weakest-motif set.
- **Endgame fundamentals** convert the most rating per hour → endgame drills when weak.

## Chess psychology (the differentiator)
- **Tilt:** frustration after a loss cascades into more losses. Recognizing and breaking
  it is vital. → "Streaky" consistency flag + "stop after two losses, reset & breathe."
- **Time pressure:** decision quality collapses on a low clock; budgeting the clock is a
  trainable skill. → "Errors under time pressure" mindset habit (clock budgeting).
- **Focus & stamina:** simple blunders come from attention drops, even when winning.
  → blunder-check habit; "switch modes when winning."
- **Consistency is a mental skill**, measurable as the spread of per-game accuracy.
  → **Consistency score** (std-dev of game accuracy) as a focus/tilt proxy.
- **Mindset matters:** calm, present, growth-oriented players improve faster.
  → mindset signals reframe each mistake as the habit that fixes it.

## How it maps to the product
- **Roster** — diagnosis (snapshot): phases, repeated mistakes, rating trend.
- **Curriculum** — the plan: ranked focus areas, drills on real blunders, weekly routine.
- **Analytics** — deep dive: growth curve, consistency (focus/tilt proxy), mindset signals.
